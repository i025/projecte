# Trusted Blender pilot: public subset

All RGB, Geometry, component Masks and PNGs come from the existing first-party source-controlled primitive fixture, actually executed in this pilot. No BlenderWorld reference photo, historical Agent Render, saved .blend, model weight or third-party scene asset is published.

Public JSON/Markdown copies redact private absolute paths and the local Blender executable location; metadata paths become repository-relative provenance. PNG EXIF/render text is removed with identical decoded pixels and unchanged image/color data chunks. Raw server files remain untouched. No measurements, matrices, frozen evidence, review flags, answers, failures or Unknown/N/A states were changed.

The controller observes its own whitelisted fixture launches and same-run artifacts. execution_chain.json is an audit trail, not a cryptographic certificate or proof against later edits. The legacy launch field scene_validity=valid denotes render execution success only. Static replay remains unverified; all evaluations retain reviewed=false, provisional/debug, formal_eligible=false. Runtime stays blocked for unattended Agent execution.

Raw Depth/Normal/ID NPY, EXR passes, Blender logs, resource/process JSONL and private child caches/config remain local. Their summaries and relevant Mask PNG/Geometry/metadata are included. Local-only array names in the audit trail are provenance, not public download links; the controlled renderer regenerates them. Duplicate per-evaluation comparison PNGs are omitted; the primary comparison.png remains included. No VLM or other model call was added.

Original vlm_adapter_integration files and their intentional raw/public working-tree differences are excluded from this commit. Legacy unbound VLM answers are not retroactively edited or assigned to this fixture. Explicit Mock/replay image-path declarations only establish a diagnostic association, not visual verification or content authentication.

Files are staged from reviewed public copies, preserving local originals; local metadata/PNGs may intentionally differ from their committed sanitized versions. Do not indiscriminately stage those differences. Reproduction and the real-vs-Oracle test distinction are in summary.md. No signatures, hashes, registry, new Energy or extra scoring rule were introduced.
